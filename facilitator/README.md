# NEAR exact facilitator (reference)

The FastNEAR reference facilitator at https://x402.mikedotexe.com settles a NEAR `exact` delegate payment and names `x402-relayer2.mike.near` as its `near:mainnet` signer. This process does those settlement checks and also verifies and records a delivery receipt at `POST /receipt` (`match`, `content_mismatch`, `payment_mismatch`, `bad_signature`, or `unanswered`) without submitting another transaction. `near-sandbox` and `nearup` are not on `PATH` here, so the chain it talks to is an in-process mock of the JSON-RPC methods in the NEAR exact scheme (`block`, `query`, `broadcast_tx_commit`) on `127.0.0.1`. The signed delegate in this reference is canonical JSON, not the Borsh `SignedDelegateAction` a production facilitator verifies, and this process does not connect to mainnet.
